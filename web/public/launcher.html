only chatz

a floaty little chatroom — everything below is already inside

pick a name, say hello. the room runs on its own server, messages arrive over a live socket, and the first account ever created becomes the owner.

enter the room open in a new tab save the one-file room

username and password only — no email, no verification. the one-file room is the whole app in a single html file, stamped with this server's address so it opens from disk too.

rooms

  • #general say hi and hang out
  • #announcements everyone reads, only the owner posts
  • #random off topic, always
  • #vip opens when the vip flair is yours
  • #mvp opens when the mvp flair is yours

people

  • direct messages friends stay pinned at the top of the sidebar
  • who's online a live board of everyone and the room they float in
  • typing notes see who is answering before the line lands
  • profiles name, @username, pronouns, bio, accent, cropped avatar, member number and a banner theme
  • names painted by rank and flair — owner, co-owner, mvp, vip

roles

  • the ladder owner › co-owner › admin › mod › member, enforced by the server
  • who promotes only the owner hands out roles
  • moderation mute, kick and ban; a mute stops posting, a ban stops signing in
  • payday gaining mod or admin pays 10,000 coins once per role

messages

  • editing your own lines, marked “(edited)”
  • replies quote a line from the same room
  • reactions six emoji, tap yours again to take it back
  • pictures send an image with or without words
  • deleting your own — the owner may remove anyone's
  • mentions @username pings the room they are in

flairs & the daily wheel

  • the wheel one turn every 24 hours; banked pass spins skip the wait
  • wearing own them forever, wear three at once
  • the rare ones vip and mvp open their rooms; custom is a tag you write
  • the shop buy flairs with coins — 10,000, then 20,000, then 30,000, one rung at a time

  • leaderboard the ten richest purses, live
