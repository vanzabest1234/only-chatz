MATHS · FRACTIONS

Adding Fractions with Different Denominators

Rule: give both fractions the same denominator before you add them.

1/2 + 1/3 = 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6
  1. The lowest common denominator of 2 and 3 is 6.
  2. Rewrite each fraction in sixths.
  3. Add the numerators: 3 + 2 = 5.

Practice: try 3/4 + 1/6.

SCIENCE · BIOLOGY

Photosynthesis

Key idea: green plants use light to turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose, releasing oxygen.

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen
  • Chlorophyll in the leaves captures light.
  • Carbon dioxide enters through tiny pores in the leaf.
  • Oxygen leaves the plant as a by-product.

Check: which gas do plants give off during photosynthesis?

ENGLISH · GRAMMAR

Making Subjects and Verbs Agree
