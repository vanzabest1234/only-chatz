Quick Lesson Notes

MATHS · FRACTIONS

Adding Unlike Denominators

Rule: Rewrite both fractions using a common denominator before adding.

1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12
  1. Find a common denominator: 12.
  2. Convert each fraction to twelfths.
  3. Add the numerators: 4 + 3 = 7.

Practice: Solve 2/5 + 1/3.

SCIENCE · BIOLOGY

Photosynthesis

Key idea: Green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water.

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen
  • Chlorophyll absorbs light energy.
  • Carbon dioxide enters through leaf stomata.
  • Oxygen is released as a product.

Check: Name the pigment that absorbs light.

ENGLISH · GRAMMAR

Subject–Verb Agreement

Rule: A singular subject normally takes a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb.

✓ The student writes. ✓ The students write.

Ignore words in a prepositional phrase when identifying the main subject.

Practice: “The box of pencils ___ on the desk.” Choose is or are.
